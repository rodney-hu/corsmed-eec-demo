Questions

Who is this course for?

Program directors, instructors and clinical educators who teach MRI students or new technologists.

Do I need a simulator to use the fixes?

No. Each fix works with the images, cases and practice time your program already has. Day 5 shows how a simulator can make practice repeatable.

Can simulation replace clinical hours?

No. Day 2 covers what a practice environment does well, and what only time with patients and a real scanner can teach.

Will this fit my current curriculum?

Yes. Each day is one change you can try in your next class, not a new syllabus.

Is this safety training?

No. It's educational content for instructors and doesn't replace your program's MRI safety training or site protocols.

How much time does it take?

About 4 minutes a day for 5 days.
